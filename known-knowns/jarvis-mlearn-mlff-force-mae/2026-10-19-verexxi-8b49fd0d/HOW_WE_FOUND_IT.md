Brief: beat the best listed Li force MAE (snap_mlearn, 0.02734 eV/A) on the 29 official test structures by at least 5%, from the 241 train structures only. Li forces are small (zero-force MAE on the test ids 0.206 eV/A, versus 0.95 for Mo), the cells range from 2 to 54 atoms, and the set mixes ab initio MD snapshots of 53- and 54-atom bcc cells with small strained or defected cells. The metric reproduction and the val rule (every tenth train id) were the ones built for the Mo package and were not changed. The training recipe (batch size 3, learning rate 0.005, 150 epochs, 2 interaction layers, r_max 4.5) was taken over from what had fixed under-fitting on Mo; only the width was selected for Li on val: 16 channels gave a val MAE of 0.010867 and 32 channels 0.010175, so 32 won under the frozen rule. Three seeds on all 241 train ids then gave test MAEs of 0.013018, 0.013097 and 0.013092 (spread 4e-5), ensemble 0.012793; the seeds agree so closely that averaging gains only about 2%. The model is far below the listed SNAP value and also below the 0.0264 that a later paper (ALIGNN 2.0) lists. What did not happen: no linear ACE, MTP or kernel model was tried (no Windows build), and no energy or stress claim is made.
