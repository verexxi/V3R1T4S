Brief: beat the best listed Si force MAE (alignnff_mlearn_wt1, 0.06942 eV/A; the leaderboard also lists a NequIP entry at 0.0901) on the 25 official test structures by at least 5%, from the 214 train structures only. The Si set holds diamond-cubic cells of 24 to 64 atoms: molecular-dynamics snapshots at several temperatures plus strained and defected cells, with a zero-force test MAE of 0.566 eV/A. The metric reproduction, the val rule (every tenth train id) and the training recipe (batch size 3, learning rate 0.005, 150 epochs, r_max 4.5, two interaction layers) were taken over unchanged from the Mo and Li work; for Si only the narrow model was run, because the 8-hour budget could not pay for a width search: 16 channels reached a val MAE of 0.051444, already below the 0.0659 bar, so that width was used with no comparison against a wider one. Three seeds on all 214 train ids then gave test MAEs of 0.052529, 0.052342 and 0.052319, ensemble 0.049536 (about 5% better than a single seed). Because only one width was tried, the gap to a possibly better wider model is unknown; nothing about energies or stresses is claimed. A later paper (ALIGNN 2.0) lists 0.0872 for this task, above this result.
